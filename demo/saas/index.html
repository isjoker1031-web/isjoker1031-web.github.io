01 · FEATURES

You still sound like you. It just stops eating your evenings.

Fielded doesn't pretend to be a whole support team — it just quietly clears the 80% of tickets that don't need a founder's judgment.

Auto-triage

Every ticket gets tagged, prioritized, and matched to past conversations in under a second.

Drafts in your voice

Trained on your last 100 replies, so drafts sound like you on a good day — not a script.

Escalation rules

Refunds, angry tone, or a whiff of an enterprise deal — you set the tripwires, we watch for them.

Sunday digest

One email: what got resolved, what's trending, and the three tickets worth reading yourself.

02 · HOW IT WORKS

Set up on a coffee break.

No migration, no import script — Fielded sits in front of the inbox you already use.

Point it at your inbox

Forward support@ to Fielded, or connect Gmail/Helpscout in two clicks.

Feed it your past replies

It reads your last 100 sent replies once, to learn your tone — nothing generic.

Approve, then trust it

Review drafts for a week if you want. Most people turn on auto-send by day three.

05 · FAQ

Only once you turn auto-send on for a given category. Everything starts in review-only mode.

Tone detection routes it straight to "needs you" — no draft, no delay, just a ping.

Can I export everything if I leave?

One-click export of every ticket and draft, any time. No lock-in.
